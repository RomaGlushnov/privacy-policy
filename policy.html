Information Collection and Use

The game itself does not directly collect personal user data on our own servers. Local game progress is saved on your device.

Google Account Sign-In and Cloud Saving

We plan to integrate Google Play Games Services (GPGS), allowing you to sign in with your Google Account. Your game progress, scores, and settings may be saved to Google's cloud servers. We do not have direct access to your Google password. You can manage or delete your cloud save data through your Google Account settings.
