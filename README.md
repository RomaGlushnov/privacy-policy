Privacy Policy for Flappy Bird - Revival

Gothesk Games built the Flappy Bird - Revival app as a Free app. This SERVICE is provided by Gothesk Games at no cost and is intended for use as is.

Information Collection and Use
The game itself does not directly collect personal user data on our own servers. Local game progress is saved on your device.

Google Account Sign-In and Cloud Saving
We plan to integrate GPGS (Google Play Games Services — игровые сервисы Google Play), allowing you to sign in with your Google Account. By doing so, your game progress, scores, and settings will be saved to Google's cloud servers. This ensures your data is preserved even if you uninstall the app or change devices. We do not have direct access to your Google password. You can manage or delete your cloud save data at any time through your Google Account settings.

Third-Party Services and Advertising
We use or plan to use third-party advertising networks, such as Google AdMob and Unity Ads. These services may collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID) and IP (Internet Protocol — межсетевой протокол) addresses to serve relevant ads.

Links to the privacy policies of third-party service providers:

Google Play Services & AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Unity Ads: https://unity3d.com/legal/privacy-policy

Children’s Privacy
This app is designed to be safe for users of all ages, including children under 13 years of age. We do not intentionally collect personally identifiable information from children. Advertising networks used in this app are configured to serve contextual (non-personalized) ads to users identified as children.
